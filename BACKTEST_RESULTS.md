# ARS SPORT Prediction Engine — Backtest Results

The ARS SPORT prediction engine was backtested against Premier League historical results across three completed seasons.

| Season | ARS SPORT Accuracy | Historical Baseline | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2023/24 | 48.33% | 44.72% | +3.61 pp |
| 2024/25 | 46.94% | 39.44% | +7.50 pp |
| 2025/26 | 44.17% | 42.22% | +1.95 pp |

### Combined

- Matches evaluated: 1,080
- ARS SPORT accuracy: 46.48%
- Historical baseline accuracy: 42.13%
- Difference: +4.35 percentage points
- ARS SPORT Brier score: 0.6325
- Historical baseline Brier score: 0.6552

## Interpretation

The backtest shows that the ARS SPORT model outperformed the selected historical baseline in each tested season.

Historical backtest performance does not guarantee future prediction results.

The model should be treated as a statistical decision-support system rather than a guarantee of match outcomes.
